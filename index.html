Featured work

Agricope POS

A multi-tenant point of sale for shops and restaurants in Qatar: one deployment, many businesses, each with branches, brands, staff on 4-digit PINs, a catalogue, cash drawers and bilingual English–Arabic bills. Any browser becomes a till.

  • RoleLead developer
  • WhenAug 2026 – present
  • TeamThree developers
  • StatusV2 on staging, V1 live

Screenshots from the system's demo business with generated sales history. The codebase is private and commercial.

What I built

  • The platform, end to end: register and split payments, receipts and barcode scanning, tables and tabs, a kitchen display, customers and credit, cash drawers with X and Z reports, refunds, stock and recipe costing.
  • Multi-tenancy: every business sees only its own rows; the business id comes from the token, never from the request.
  • Offline selling: the till keeps taking cash and card with the Wi-Fi down, queues sales in IndexedDB and sends them when it reconnects, each with its own id so nothing is counted twice.
  • V2, the redesign: every screen rebuilt to the Figma file across till, office and phone layouts, with feature switches, a till lock, loyalty points, own delivery and per-brand prices — and nothing V1 did dropped.

Engineering I'm proud of

  • Money that never drifts: amounts are decimal strings end to end, every sum runs through one shared module the till and the API both compile, and the server prices every order itself.
  • Fixing a live outage: payments and approvals failed in the browser because the API's CORS list refused two headers. Every header the app sends now lives in one shared list, with a test on each side.
  • Approvals bound to the exact request: a manager approves from their phone or a PIN pad, and the token only works for that one discount, void or refund.
  • A security pass: locked tills revoke their tokens on the server, wrong PINs are counted per business, and a customer's e-receipt link shows only what the bill prints.
